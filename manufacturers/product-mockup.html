How it was found

  1. Read the invoice from QuickBooks.
  2. Read the customer PO PDF from the email inbox.
  3. Compared both to the quote in the orders sheet.
  4. Two of three agree, so it drafted the fix and stopped.

Evidence

Customer PO #88417, line 1, unit price $20.25. Received by email Sept 9. Attached to Q-2291 in the orders sheet.

Proposed fix (drafted, not applied) ▲Destructive

Credit memo for $2,640 against INV-5510, then reissue at $20.25. It changes a posted invoice, so it asks every time.

Rule 1

Reads run without asking. Writes ask once per group. Changing a posted record asks every time.

Rule 2

Every change shows evidence and waits for your approval.

Rule 3

Every action is logged with who, what, and where. Undo works any time, except for sent messages.
