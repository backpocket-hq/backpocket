Questions

What owners and office managers ask first.

My bookkeeper already handles this.

Then she keeps handling it, with less chasing. Backpocket drafts the reminders and watches the dates. She reviews and approves, and nothing changes in your accounting software unless she wants it to.

I don't want anyone in my accounting system.

You don't have to give access to start. The free check works from documents you send. If we go further, access starts read-only and you can end it any time.

What if it gets something wrong?

Every draft and every number shows where it came from, what we assumed, and what's missing. Nothing goes out until you approve it, so a wrong draft costs you a click, not a relationship.

My GCs know me. I don't want to burn the relationship.

Messages are short and friendly, and they go out under your name only after you approve them.

What does it cost?

The first check is free. If you want to go further, we'll talk price once we've seen your numbers.
