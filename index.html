What owners and controllers ask first.

What would it do for me that I'm doing now that I wouldn't have to?

Today you create the same information in several sheets and check it by hand. Backpocket takes each fact once, keeps every sheet in agreement, and flags drift with the signed document attached. You approve each fix.

We're close to outgrowing QuickBooks. Do we have to replace it?

No. QuickBooks stays and it is never changed. Your spreadsheets stay too. Backpocket works on top of them, so you can decide on your own timeline.

Can I still control who sees which spreadsheet?

Yes. Each view keeps its own file and its own permissions, so each person sees only what you want them to see.

I hate getting automated messages. Will you send them to my customers?

No. Nothing goes to your customers or GCs. Drafts go only to your own vendors and team, such as your insurance agent, and only after you approve them.

We don't have time for a learning curve.

It takes about 30 minutes of your time to start. You send three exports, and we work on copies. You do not need to log in to anything.

What if it gets something wrong?

Every flag shows where it came from. Nothing changes until you approve it, so a wrong flag costs you a click.
